The semantic layer

Think about the data, not where it lives.

What does the data mean? How does it relate? What view do you need?

DataSong discovers schemas and metadata, infers relationships and business meaning across systems, and builds a semantic graph that users can browse and chat with. Databases, query engines, storage systems and pipelines stay underneath as implementation details.

Analysts and ML engineers can use English directly over this layer to combine data from different systems, express filters, joins, transformations and derived fields, and construct the views they need for analysis or model development.

Just ask

One request. Data from wherever it lives.

“Give me customers in India who purchased in the last 12 months, their latest support interaction and lifetime revenue.”

Customer data may be in PostgreSQL, transactions in the warehouse and support events in S3. DataSong works out how the pieces relate and builds the governed view.

“Show me the hourly temperature, vibration and power history for machines that had a maintenance event in the last 30 days.”

Time-series data may be in Delta tables on S3, maintenance in an operations DB, and asset metadata elsewhere. DataSong aligns and combines them into a usable view.

No rip-and-replace

Your systems remain your systems.

DataSong sits across the databases, warehouses, lakehouses, applications and object stores an organization already uses. It federates data directly wherever practical instead of requiring the data estate to be rebuilt around another central platform.

If a view becomes heavily reused, needs predictable performance or is cheaper to materialize, DataSong can turn it into a managed snapshot or repeatable pipeline.

How it works

From scattered systems to a usable view.

  • 1. ConnectExisting SQL, NoSQL, warehouse, lakehouse, application and object-store systems.
  • 2. DiscoverSchemas, tables, fields, metadata and available data assets.
  • 3. UnderstandInfer relationships and business meaning and build the semantic graph.
  • 4. ExploreBrowse or chat with the semantic layer to understand what exists.
  • 5. AskDescribe the required data view in plain English.
  • 6. BuildCombine, filter, join, derive and reshape the relevant data.
Governed by design

Easy to ask doesn't mean open to everything.

DataSong controls who can discover and access data, who can create views and pipelines, and which underlying sources each person or application is permitted to use. The same access controls apply whether a result is queried dynamically or materialized for repeated use.
