the problem

The questions are simple.
Getting the right data to answer them isn't.

You know the business process and what you want to understand. But getting the data means navigating tables, SQL, warehouses, lakehouses, telemetry, schemas and pipelines — and translating back and forth with data teams to work out what actually represents the business.

Weeks become months. Costs grow. Data gets copied. Pipelines and views multiply. Storage and compute multiply with them.

what lemap changes

lemap learns that translation for you.

It continuously learns how the processes that run your business connect to the data they create and use.

business process→business objects→enterprise data→learning enterprise map
Manufacturingproduction → quality → shipment
Healthcarepatient → treatment → outcome
Financetransaction → settlement → ledger
why that matters

So when you need data, lemap already knows where to look.

It can identify what data is needed, whether it already exists, and eventually whether it can be reused or federated on demand instead of copied into yet another pipeline.
