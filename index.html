The problem

Enterprises have thousands of tables.
Finding the right data is still manual.

Data is scattered across systems, teams and formats. Engineers spend time discovering schemas, tracing relationships, moving data and maintaining intermediate copies before analysis can even begin.

01

Data is fragmented

SQL, NoSQL, warehouses, Delta lakes, S3 and operational systems all expose different pieces of the picture.

02

Knowledge is fragmented too

Knowing which table means what often lives in documentation, tribal knowledge or the heads of a few engineers.

03

Every new use case creates more plumbing

New analytics and ML workloads often lead to another ETL job, another copy and another pipeline to maintain.

How DataSong works

From business intent to governed data view.

DataSong uses AI to understand your data estate, resolve natural-language requests into data sources, and generate distributed query plans across those systems.

ConnectUse existing databases, warehouses, lakes and object stores.
UnderstandBuild a semantic map of schemas, data and relationships.
AskDescribe the data view you need in plain English.
ExecuteFederate queries directly across sources using distributed SQL.
GovernEnforce who can see data and who can create or operate pipelines.
Federate first

Move less data.

DataSong avoids maintaining unnecessary intermediate copies. Where possible, it builds views directly from source systems. Where materialization is useful for performance, cost or repeatability, DataSong can maintain snapshots in target stores such as S3.

Federated views

Query data where it already lives instead of copying everything into yet another platform.

Materialize when useful

Create and refresh snapshots only when economics, latency or workload characteristics justify them.

Scale compute with demand

Run distributed query plans on horizontally scalable compute and scale resources in or out as workloads change.
