the learning enterprise map

Your business knows what it wants to understand.
Finding the data shouldn't take months.

Business processes run across applications, databases and telemetry. Turning a business question into the right data still means repeatedly working out where that data lives and how it connects.

the problem

The process is understood.
The data that represents it often isn't.

Analytics, ML and data engineering teams spend weeks tracing data, joining systems and rebuilding views — often duplicating pipelines, storage and compute that already exist.

Manufacturingproduction → quality → shipment
Healthcarepatient → treatment → outcome
Financetransaction → settlement → ledger
what lemap changes

lemap learns the connection between the processes that run your business and the data that represents them.

It builds an evolving map of business processes, enterprise data and the relationships between them.

business process→business objects→enterprise data→learning enterprise map
why that matters

So the data you need can be found, reused or assembled on demand.

lemap can determine what data a requested view needs, where it already exists, and whether it can be reused or federated instead of copied again.
